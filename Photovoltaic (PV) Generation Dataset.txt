his dataset contains hourly photovoltaic (PV) generation data and associated input variables collected from an experimental rooftop PV system.

The dataset includes temporal, meteorological, and orientation-related features used for short-term PV power forecasting. Measurements were recorded at an hourly resolution over approximately one year.

The dataset consists of the following variables:

- Day: Day of the year
- Cosine of Day: Cyclical representation of the day of the year
- Hour: Hour of the day
- Cosine of Hour: Cyclical representation of the hour of the day
- Air Temperature (°C)
- Dew Point (°C)
- Wind Speed (m/s)
- Air Pressure (hPa)
- Weather Condition: Categorical weather description
- Solar Radiation (W/m²)

For each PV panel (PV-1 to PV-5), the following variables are provided:
- Cosine value of the solar incidence angle
- Measured PV power output (W or Wh)

Additionally:
- Total PV Power: Aggregated power output of all panels

The PV system consists of multiple panels with different tilt and azimuth configurations, enabling the analysis of orientation-aware PV forecasting under identical environmental conditions.

This repository includes raw and processed data along with variable descriptions to support further research in photovoltaic forecasting and energy systems.
